Seu Voto

Follow the politicians you voted for.

Seu Voto tracks Brazilian Congress activity with official data — presence, bills and roll-call votes of Chamber of Deputies and Senate members — and explains every decision in plain language. No accounts, no tracking: the watchlist lives on the user's device.

  • Official sources only (Câmara & Senado open data), refreshed daily
  • Bill summaries and a blind political quiz, reviewed and source-linked
  • Installable PWA with push notifications — no sign-up required
